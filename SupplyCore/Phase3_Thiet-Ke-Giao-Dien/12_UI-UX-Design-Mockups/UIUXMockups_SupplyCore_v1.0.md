## __2\.1 Core design principles__

__Nguyen tac__

__Mo ta__

__Vi du ap dung trong SupplyCore__

Thong tin quan trong hien thi ro rang

__Role\-adaptive__

Giao dien thich ung vai tro

Executive thay KPI tong; Storekeeper thay ton kho chi tiet

Ngan nguoi dung lam sai

Disable button khi chua du dieu kien; confirm dialog cho hanh dong huy

Giam so click de hoan thanh task

Quick actions tren list view; keyboard shortcuts cho form

__Trust & auditability__

Nguoi dung tin tuong du lieu

Hien thi nguoi tao, thoi gian, trang thai workflow ro rang

__Hospital context__

Phu hop moi truong y te

Mau sac phong tinh, font de doc, khong phan tam
